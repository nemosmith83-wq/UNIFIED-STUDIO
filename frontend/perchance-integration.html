Why they are not called from the app

The published Perchance Terms of Service prohibit scripted or bot access. The official pages reviewed describe plugins for use within Perchance generators; they do not document a supported API for a separate app. Unified Studio therefore does not call undocumented endpoints, automate the pages, or claim an external generation succeeded.
